01 / Approach

Shade, breeze and green.

Accra is hot and humid most of the year. Good architecture here works with the climate instead of fighting it, so buildings are comfortable and cheaper to run.

A.1Deep shade

Generous roofs and overhangs keep the sun off walls and windows, so rooms stay cool.

A.2Natural breeze

Openings placed for cross-ventilation let air flow through, so you rely less on air conditioning.

A.3Living green

Courtyards, palms and planting bring life, privacy and cooler air right up to the house.

We also encourage clients to harvest rainwater and to use solar and wind energy, so buildings can power and cool themselves.

Client note:"Well shaded. Natural ventilation. A lot of green… I love it."

04 / Practice

Albert Essamuah Associates is listed by the Architects Registration Council of Ghana among the architecture firms in good standing, every year since at least 2019. Managing Director Albert William Stanhope Essamuah, FGIA, is a former university lecturer and has consulted for the Government of Ghana, the World Bank, the African Development Bank, UNHCR, the Christian Council of Ghana and the Methodist Church of Ghana.
